04 A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s make
things clear.

A partnership should start with
an honest conversation.

Who is a PROtv partnership for?+

Greensboro and Triad businesses, community organizations, and brands interested in independent film, music, and culture. You don’t need a finished campaign idea to explore a fit.

How much does a partnership cost?+

Pricing is discussed after we understand your goals, budget, timing, and desired scope. These options are conversation starters, not fixed packages or a purchase offer. In-kind ideas can also be explored.

What audience or results can you promise?+

PROtv is growing. We don’t promise a particular number of views, sales, or returns. Ask for current, verified audience information during the discussion, and agree in advance on the reporting available for your campaign.

Can we sponsor a specific film or creator?+

You can propose an idea. Any association depends on creator approval, content availability, rights, and a written agreement. Sponsorship does not grant editorial control or rights to a creator’s work.

What happens when I save my interest?+

Your draft stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is emailed or submitted to PROtv, no payment is collected, and no partnership is booked. You can download your brief, edit it, or clear the saved draft.
