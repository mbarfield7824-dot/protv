# PROtv rights matrix (title by title)

**Investigation of 2026-10-24. Not a legal opinion. No title is represented as
legally cleared beyond the documents cited.** This file supersedes the counts
in rights-launch-classification.md. Nothing in production was changed: no
title was restored, restricted or edited.

## Method

1. **Records:** production Firestore `videos` and `titleRightsEvidence`, read
   only (owner-authorized; `.get()` calls only); store/RIGHTS-REPORT.md;
   store/evidence/rights-evidence.md; poster rollout records in
   audit-reports/ and the legacy-poster branch.
2. **Source copy:** 37 titles were uploaded straight to Mux, with no source
   file recorded. Each PROtv runtime (Mux duration) was compared against
   public Wikimedia Commons and Internet Archive files. A match within 2
   seconds identifies the probable source item. It is evidence, not proof.
3. **What PROtv actually streams:** opening and closing frames of PROtv's own
   copies were read through Mux's public thumbnail API (no video segments).
   Restoration cards, distributor logos, colour, burned-in subtitles and short
   titles were checked this way. This found several copies that are commercial
   restorations or disc releases, which the earlier review could not see.
4. **Public records:** Wikipedia, Wikidata, Commons license review and the
   Copyright Office online records (1978 onward), as cited in
   rights-evidence.md. Printed Catalog of Copyright Entries renewals (before
   1978) were not searched. An archive upload or IMDb entry is never counted
   as proof.

"Documented clearance" means the documents cover both the work **and** the
copy PROtv streams, with no unlicensed added music or restoration material.
It does not cover the poster, which has its own column.

**High-risk titles** (counsel review recommended before launch; restricting
them is the owner's decision and was not done): Body and Soul (1925); Carnival of Souls (1962); Cavalcade of Stars; Detour (1945); One Step Beyond - The Storm; The Scar of Shame (1927); Within Our Gates (1920); Woman on the Run (1950).

## Cross-cutting findings

- **Commercial restorations in the stream.** Body and Soul (Criterion),
  Carnival of Souls (Criterion/Janus), Detour (2018 Academy Film Archive
  restoration), Woman on the Run (UCLA preservation) and KIPHO (Deutsche
  Kinemathek) all carry the restorer's or publisher's logo in PROtv's copy.
  Whether a restoration of a public-domain film adds protectable material is a
  question for counsel. Separately, distributor logos should not appear in
  PROtv's stream, and a modern commissioned score is protected in its own
  right.
- **Music compositions.** A film's lapsed copyright does not free songs or
  scores that were published and renewed separately; that was the basis of
  the two Virgil Thomson restrictions. Gulliver's Travels, The Duke Is Tops,
  The Bronze Buckaroo, Jungle Book and the 1932 Merrie Melodies short raise
  the same question.
- **Posters.** The 32 owner-supplied posters have no recorded creation
  method, and two depict real performers. One poster is a hotlinked Google
  Images thumbnail. The owner should record how each poster was made; check
  AI-assisted posters against the tool's commercial-use terms.
- **Catalog accuracy (proposals; nothing was edited).** "Looney Tunes
  Classics: Collection" is the single short "Dumb Patrol" (1931). "Merri
  Melodies shorts" is "You're Too Careless with Your Kisses!" (1932).
  "Superman cartoons" is dated 1994, which is the CD-ROM year.
