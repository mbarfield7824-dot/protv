## Why Apple and Samsung LIVE stay low
About 70% of program time is U.S. public-domain classics. Roku and the website accept them on evidence about the film
itself. Apple and Samsung require the soundtrack and the source copy to be reviewed as well (the owner's evidence
standard), and that review has not been done. This is an evidence gap, not a contract restriction or an outdated record.
